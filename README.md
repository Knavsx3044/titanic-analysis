## What the data actually shows

If you were a woman and rich, you were almost guaranteed to survive.
Being a woman in a man's world gives you an edge — and money gives 
you influence. Those two things combined were basically a survival 
guarantee on the Titanic.

- Women survived at 74% vs 19% for men
- First class survival was nearly double third class
- Even poor women outlived rich men

The data doesn't lie — gender and wealth decided who lived.
